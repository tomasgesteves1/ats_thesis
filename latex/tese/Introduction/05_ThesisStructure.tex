\section{Thesis Structure}
\label{sec:intro_structure}

% How is the rest of the thesis organised?
The rest of this thesis is organised as follows.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Chapter~\ref{chapter:state_of_the_art} reviews the modelling, the control and the cooperation of autonomous vehicles, with a focus on tethered and maritime systems.
    \item Chapter~\ref{chapter:methodology} derives the models of both vehicles and formulates their \gls{nmpc} controllers.
    \item Chapter~\ref{chapter:sys_implementation} presents the simulation environment, validates the models, and describes the implementation of the controllers, the manoeuvres and missions, and the software.
    \item Chapter~\ref{chapter:results} evaluates the tracking of each controller, the landing on the moving deck under waves and wind, and the system in complete missions.
    \item Chapter~\ref{chapter:conclusions} summarises the achievements of this work and proposes future work.
\end{itemize}
