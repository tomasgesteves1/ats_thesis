\section{Contributions}
\label{sec:intro_contributions}

% What does this work contribute?
The main contributions of this work are the following.
\begin{itemize}
    \item A closed-form model of the force of a slack tether that accounts for its weight.
    \item Two cooperating \gls{nmpc} controllers, one for each vehicle, which exchange their predicted trajectories to anticipate the motion of each other within the reach of the tether.
    \item Manoeuvres for the takeoff from and the landing on the moving deck, and for the \gls{usv} to follow the \gls{uav} around a structure without the tether wrapping around it.
    \item A simulation environment of the tethered system at sea, with waves, the PX4 autopilot of the \gls{uav} and a tether whose length is set by a winch.
\end{itemize}
