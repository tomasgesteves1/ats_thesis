\section{Contributions}
\label{sec:intro_contributions}

% What does this work contribute?
The main contributions of this work are the following.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Control-oriented models of the \gls{wamv} and of the x500, validated against the simulator.
    \item Two cooperating \gls{nmpc} controllers, one for each vehicle, which exchange their predicted trajectories to keep the tether within its reach while each vehicle respects its own limits.
    \item A simulation environment of the tethered system at sea, with waves, the PX4 autopilot of the \gls{uav}, and a tether whose length is set by a winch.
    \item Manoeuvres and missions for both vehicles, including the takeoff and the landing on the moving deck, launched and followed from a Mission Control Centre.
    \item An evaluation of the system in tracking and planning, in three missions, and under waves and wind.
\end{itemize}
