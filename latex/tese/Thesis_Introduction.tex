\chapter{Introduction}
\label{chapter:introduction}

A tether between a \gls{usv} and a \gls{uav} lets the \gls{uav} share the endurance of the \gls{usv}, but it also binds the motion of each vehicle to that of the other. This thesis treats this bond not as a constraint to be avoided, but as the basis of a cooperation in which the two vehicles operate as a single system.
