\chapter{Results}
\label{chapter:results}

% What is shown in this chapter, and under which conditions?
The system of Chapter~\ref{chapter:sys_implementation} is evaluated in simulation in three steps of growing scope: the tracking and planning of each controller, complete missions with both vehicles, and the response to waves and wind. Unless stated otherwise, the results were obtained with the default sea of the simulator, which moves the winch up and down by about $0.25\,\text{m}$, without wind, and with the final configuration of both controllers, given in Section~\ref{sec:controller_implementation}.
