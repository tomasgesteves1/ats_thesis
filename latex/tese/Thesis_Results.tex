\chapter{Results}
\label{chapter:results}

% What is shown in this chapter, and under which conditions?
This chapter evaluates the system of Chapter~\ref{chapter:sys_implementation} in simulation, first in the tracking and planning of each controller, then in complete missions, and finally under waves and wind. Unless stated otherwise, the results were obtained with the default sea of the simulator, which moves the winch up and down by about $0.25\,\text{m}$, without wind, and with the final configuration of both controllers, given in Section~\ref{sec:controller_implementation}.
