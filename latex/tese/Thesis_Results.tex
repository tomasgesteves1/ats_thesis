\chapter{Results}
\label{chapter:results}

% How is the system evaluated?
The evaluation starts with each controller on its own, following simple references and finding a feasible motion when the reference lies beyond the reach of the tether or the limits of the vehicle. The two vehicles then work together in three example missions, from the takeoff to the landing on the moving deck. Finally, the same manoeuvres are repeated under growing waves and wind.
