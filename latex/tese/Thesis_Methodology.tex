\chapter{Methodology}
\label{chapter:methodology}

This chapter presents the methodology developed for the cooperative control of the tethered \gls{usv}--\gls{uav} system. Section~\ref{sec:system_modeling} derives the models of both vehicles and of the force that the tether applies on the \gls{uav}, and Section~\ref{sec:system_control} formulates the two cooperating \gls{nmpc} controllers that use these models to drive each vehicle within its limits while keeping the tether within its reach.
